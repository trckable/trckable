---
title: "Funnels"
description: "Build a funnel from pages and goals in Explore and see where people drop off."
---

A funnel is the steps people take, in order, and how many get to each one. Build
it in Explore, under Goals.

## Build one

The funnel is one card. Steps are numbered chips, for example
`1 Home page → 2 /docs → 3 Opened pricing`.

1. Choose **+ Add step**. A search lists your pages with their icons.
2. Pick a page, or use the flag button on a row to add it as a goal.
3. Repeat, then choose **Done**.

Remove a step with the × on its chip. A new funnel counts from today on, and
the card says so beside Done.

An empty funnel shows one line and **+ Add step**. If you can only view the
site, you see the line and no button.
