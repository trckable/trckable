---
title: "Time & currency"
description: "Set the time zone and currency your numbers are shown in."
---

Time & currency is a step in the setup checklist. Confirm both once and the
step is done.

- **Time zone** decides where a day starts and ends. Today, yesterday and each
  day in a chart follow it.
- **Currency** is the one revenue is shown in. A payment in another currency
  is converted at the rate of the day it was paid.

The step counts as done once the time zone or
currency is not UTC/USD, or you confirmed them.
