  kubectl get secret {{ include "trckable.secretName" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.setup-token}' | base64 -d
The same link is also in the log: kubectl logs deploy/{{ include "trckable.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
