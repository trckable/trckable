The Briefing's "Last 7 days" chart no longer draws the dashed line of the week before from the wrong buckets: the earlier week is now read by the hour like the line over it, so the dashed line runs the whole chart on the same scale and the change and the hover figure compare an hour with an hour. A comparison is only drawn when both periods are in buckets of the same size, and a small key names the solid line "This period" and the dashed one "Previous".
