Share is a studio on one screen that never scrolls: pick where the card goes (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Bluesky, a file or a link), pick a card (people, what changed, best day, each milestone, sources, Seen in Google, top page, leaderboard), style it (six backgrounds, six colours, kept for the site) and post it with the post written, download it or copy a link; each place gets the card at its own size.
