A site's domain can be changed in Settings → General (owners and admins): the site keeps its id, snippet and history, the old address still counts visits for 30 days (with a "Stop now"), the uptime check, status page and share links follow the new address, a note on the chart says when it changed, and Settings says plainly if the connected Search Console property is not the new domain's.
A domain changes once a day at most, the other owners are told when it does, a visit from the old address is not counted as a referral, and the sites list carries the old addresses still accepted.
