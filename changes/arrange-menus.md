The site menu is arranged with one drag list that a team menu shares: a grip at the left of each row (always there on a phone, where it is the handle), the whole row draggable with a mouse, a soft lift and a thin line where it lands, the list scrolling when held at its edge, Escape to cancel, and Alt + ↑/↓ with "Moved X to position N" said aloud. Every row has the same layout (name, then one quiet status) and the selected one is a soft fill only. A build with several teams can pass `onReorder` to let the team menu be arranged the same way.
