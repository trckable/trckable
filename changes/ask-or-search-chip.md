Where the build has Peek, the header chip and the phone's third tab read "Ask or search" again and open ⌘K; without Peek they stay Explore. The chip has a soft fill and no border. In ⌘K, the Ask Peek row leads for a question or a request (a "?", a question or request word in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian or Dutch, four words that match nothing, or a figure or date with a metric), and otherwise sits last, one arrow away.
