Live's globe is the real Earth: NASA's satellite picture by day and the lights of the cities by night, lit by the sun where it is now, in deep space with a blue atmosphere. Zooming in (up to 8x) brings sharper land and names (seas, countries, cities), places close together are one marker with their people added up, and a visitor from a known city is pinned on it. Where the picture cannot be drawn, the vector earth stays.
