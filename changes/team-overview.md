All sites is the team's overview: a strip (online now, today against usual, revenue today when there is any, sites that need attention), one line for the day, and a sortable row for each site (Attention, Traffic, Change) with its state in the site list's words, people now, a seven-day trend, today against usual, top source and revenue, a ⋯ menu (Site settings, Share, Status page, Widgets, Open) and Add a site. Every figure is read from the new `GET /api/v1/team` and left out when the server does not have it (no usual before two weeks of history, no source without a visit); the strip's totals are the sum of the rows. The old All sites page (period, View menu, Table, Uptime lens) is gone.
