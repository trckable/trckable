A viewer is offered no way to create or change anything from the ⌘K palette (no Create rows, no Settings rows), and an old ?add=site link no longer opens the add-a-site wizard for them; the server's route table now also checks that the owner gets through every route, and a role change takes effect on the person's next request.

What each role may do now lives in one table on each side (dashboard/src/lib/permissions.ts and server/internal/permissions/permissions.go), asked with `can('goal.create')` and `Can(role, action)`; the screens and handlers no longer compare a role themselves, a lint rule keeps it that way, and a shared fixture (dashboard/src/lib/permissions.json) fails the dashboard's or the server's tests if the two tables drift. A build with a role of its own adds its row with `defineRole` (dashboard) or `DefineRole` (server). No permission outcome changes.
