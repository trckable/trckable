by commenting on your first pull request as the bot asks, and it then covers
everything you contribute.

## Why there is one

The server and dashboard are AGPL-3.0 and the tracker and npm package are MIT,
and that stays so. The project also runs trckable Cloud, a hosted version, and
may one day need to offer the code under other terms (for example to a
company that cannot use the AGPL). This agreement makes that possible without
tracking down every contributor, while your contribution stays open.
