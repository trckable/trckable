# Trademarks

trckable's code is open source: the server and the dashboard under
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE), the tracker and the `trckable` npm package under
[MIT](packages/trckable/LICENSE). Use it, change it, run it, share it.

The name **trckable** and the **logo** (the ghost over its chart line) are not
covered by those licenses. They say where the official project comes from, so:

- **Running trckable, unchanged or with your own tweaks, for yourself or your
  team?** Keep the name. That is what it is.
- **Writing about it, linking to it, building something that works with it?**
  Say "trckable" freely, e.g. "a plugin for trckable".
- **Publishing a fork, or offering a changed version to others?** Please give it
  its own name and take the logo out (it lives in `dashboard/src/brand/`), so
  nobody takes your version for this one or this one for yours. Saying "based
  on trckable" is welcome.

Not sure whether something is fine? Open an issue and ask; the answer is usually yes.
